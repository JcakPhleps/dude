what he does

Not a chatbot in a box. A buddy who acts.

dude sees what you're doing and can follow through — always on your Mac, always on your say-so.

Sees your screen

Captures the display and reads what's actually there with Gemini vision — then answers in context.

Does things, not just chat

Sends a text or email, adds an event or reminder, drives Spotify — or runs a shell/AppleScript command.

Walks to your Dock

When he acts on an app, he strolls to its Dock icon, crouches with his laptop, works, then walks back.

Asks before he acts

Anything that leaves your Mac is staged for a quick y / n. Nothing fires behind your back.

Knows your stuff, on demand

Pulls in Messages, Mail, Calendar and Reminders only when your question is actually about them.

Talk or type

Click him, hit ⌃⌥D, or just talk — on-device speech turns your voice into the ask.

on your say-so

He stages it. You hit a key.

Texts, emails and commands never fire on their own. dude shows you exactly what he's about to do and waits for a single keystroke — y to send, n to drop it.

Everything runs locally. Only your screenshot and question go to the API, and only when you ask.

You: tell Gavin I'm running 10 late

Text Gavin: "running 10 late" — want me to send it?

install

Up and running in a minute.

dude is self-signed (no paid Apple account yet), so macOS asks you to confirm it once. Here's the whole dance.

Install dude
  1. Download Dude.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag dude onto Applications.
  3. If macOS says it's "damaged" or won't open, clear the quarantine flag, then launch it: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/dude.app
  4. He lands in your menu bar (his face, up top). Grant permissions as features ask.

Want real answers? dude runs in a limited stub without a key. Drop a free Google Gemini key at ~/.dude/config.json or set GOOGLE_API_KEY. The key never leaves your machine.
